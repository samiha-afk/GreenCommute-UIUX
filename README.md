# GreenCommute — UI/UX Case Study

A transportation-focused mobile application concept designed for garment factory workers in Bangladesh.

## Overview

GreenCommute was a UI/UX competition project focused on making factory transportation safer, more accessible and more predictable for garment workers.

## Problem

Factory workers face uncertainty regarding bus arrival times, route information, connectivity, boarding and transportation safety.

## Design Goals

- Make assigned bus information easy to access.
- Reduce uncertainty through arrival and delay notifications.
- Design for low-end Android devices and limited connectivity.
- Make boarding simple through a PIN-based system.
- Provide an accessible emergency alert interface.

## Design Process

- Requirements analysis
- Information architecture
- Wireframing
- High-fidelity prototyping
- Component and design system development

## Key Design Decisions

- PIN-based boarding instead of QR-first boarding.
- Home screen prioritizing bus status, ETA and SOS.
- Cached bus and route information for limited connectivity.
- High-contrast interface and large touch targets.
- Language selection during onboarding.

## Research & Limitations

Personas and user journey scenarios were developed based on the competition brief and design assumptions. Formal participant interviews and usability testing were not conducted during the competition.
